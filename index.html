The Truth About Childhood Immunisation in Australia

For years, Australia was one of the best-vaccinated countries in the world. Lately, the numbers have started to slip. This page follows the data to answer four questions: how far coverage has fallen, which vaccines, where, and how many children are missing out.

So, are we still protecting them?

  • Mostly, but less than before. Coverage has dropped below the 95% target and Australia has slipped down the world rankings.
  • It's broad, not one vaccine. Several vaccines show the same decline.
  • The 2-year check is the weak point. Every region fell at age 2.
  • Big cities hold the most unprotected children, even where percentages look fine.
